HOW TO ADD PHOTOS

Drop photos into a folder here and they show up in that gallery:

  industrial, golden, antarctic, stills, shapes, landscape, people, street
      -> the gallery with that name
  _home
      -> the 6 photos on the home page (only the first 6 are used)

- Photos show in filename order. Name them 01.jpg, 02.jpg... to choose it.
- Name a photo starting with "firstphoto" (e.g. firstphotogolden.jpg) to make
  it the gallery's cover photo. It's also shown first in the gallery.
- Delete a photo from a folder to remove it from the site.
- Make a new folder to create a new gallery.
- Any shape or size works. iPhone .heic photos work too.
- Your originals stay here and are never changed or uploaded. The website uses
  smaller copies that are made automatically in images/gallery/.

Seeing them: while preview.command is open, just refresh the page.
On the live site: open preview.command once, then in GitHub Desktop click
"Commit to main", then "Push origin".
